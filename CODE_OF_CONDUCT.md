# Code of Conduct

Rubric Studio Open follows the Contributor Covenant v2.1.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep discussion focused on improving the software and documentation.
- Assume public issues and pull requests may be read by researchers, students,
  commercial users, and maintainers from many backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of project work.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation to
protect the community and the project.
